Heart

Deco ventana / Eye catcher / Fensterdeko

January 21, 2015

A magical craft! In a jar with water you drop the wax of candles. When you have a complete layer try to get the form out. Be careful that it won´t break. Children are very surprised when they see the other site of the form. For Sant Valentin you can use cookie cutters, you get the form and put it in a circle. A really nice eye-catcher ! Thanks Sofia, Julieta, Hannah y Noah!
