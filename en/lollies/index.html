Lollies

June 13, 2014


Lollies do it yourself
Use cookie shapes and press the powdered sugar around it. Carefully removed the shapes. In a nonstick pan heat sugar and a little bit of water. If you want to have colored lolls you can also put some food coloring. (It should not be too liquid, otherwise the Lollies are not getting hard.) 
Before you let the sugar mass flow into the troughs, put shashlik skewer in each. Now wait a day.
