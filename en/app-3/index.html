How the Dragon came to our theatre

This incredible, interactive book tells the story of a very special dragon that you get to know while he is working in a theater. But who is he and how come he is working as an actor? A while ago the dragon’s environment changed, so he got scared and flew up into the sky. Before coming down, you have to choose in which country he will land: China, Scotland or the United States. Although the story only has one ending, there are three different ways to get there.
