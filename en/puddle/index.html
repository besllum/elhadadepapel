Cork boats for puddles

September 30, 2017

Raining? Time to play with water: mini boats for the puddles.

How to make them

  1. Cut a slice of cork.
  2. Push a toothpick into the middle as a mast.
  3. Add a small sail.

Tip: if the sail is too heavy, shorten the toothpick so the boat does not tip over.
