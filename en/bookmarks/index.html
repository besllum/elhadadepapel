Photo bookmarks

January 10, 2016

A bookmark that keeps your loved ones with you.

  1. Photograph the person with one or both hands up.
  2. Cut them out as neatly as you can and laminate the photo.
  3. Cut it out again leaving a few millimetres' margin and add a thread; you can tie on a pompom or tassel.

A fantastic idea from Kate's blog, Nearly Crafty. Thanks for the inspiration!
