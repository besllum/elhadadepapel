Fashion Socks I: sock doll clothes

November 13, 2015

Easy, cheap and quick doll clothes: recycling socks, no sewing!

I collect socks. That sounds strange, but next to the washing machine I keep a bag of socks that have lost their partners. At the weekend I decided to empty it and start again. Hannah wanted the socks 😳… and then she showed me why: armed only with scissors, she turned them into doll clothes while I tried to draw what she was doing. Here are the instructions. It's going to be a series!
