Butterflies with napkin antennae

May 2, 2014

  1. Cut a butterfly from a folded sheet of paper.
  2. Roll a strip of napkin between your palms into a kind of string.
  3. Wrap it around the middle of the butterfly, twist the ends and trim them to antenna length.

With a pin you can put your butterflies on flowers or a pinboard.
