Can

New use

March 11, 2015

We use wood glue for our decoupage, because it is much cheaper. This pen holder, we have made of a can and tissue paper. You lubricates the can with the glue and stuck the paper on it. When the glue has dried, lubricate  another layer of glue on the paper. The white tape on top covers the edge where you could cut yourself. Seen at Jans Schwester
