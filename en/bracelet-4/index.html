Cardboard loom bracelet

July 14, 2016

We made a mini loom from a piece of cardboard. Binding spirals keep the threads apart, and at the bottom we screwed a screw into the corrugations (so the direction of the cardboard matters) to tension the threads. But the most important thing is the Miyuki Delica beads. It's easiest to draw the pattern on paper first. Our summer is saved! :-)
