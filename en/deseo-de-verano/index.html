Summer wish in a shell

September 4, 2015

Clean a shell that still has both halves. Write your summer wish on a slip of paper and fold it until it fits inside. For the chain, braid some threads, lay them in one half and glue the two halves together with a glue gun (with an adult). Mermaid wishes!
