Owls

December 13, 2016

Owls


Owls made with pines and cotton.
With the stick of a spoon we have pressed the cotton into the holes of the pines until they are quite white.  We have cut a few circles out of cotton pads for the eyes and we have pasted them. With a rope or a wire you can tie it to the tree!
