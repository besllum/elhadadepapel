Lego head pots

September 11, 2015

From empty cream pots and large water-bottle caps we made two Lego heads. Step by step:

  1. Clean the pots and caps.
  2. Glue a cap to the centre of each lid with a glue gun (with an adult).
  3. Spray them yellow and let them dry.
  4. Draw the faces with a permanent marker.

Ready to use! I saw Lego heads made from glass jars on Beau-coup and loved them; for younger children, plastic pots are better than glass.
