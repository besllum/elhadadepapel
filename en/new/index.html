News: a new job

July 27, 2013

As many of you have noticed, I've been posting less lately. I have a new job! I work for a construction company in Valencia that works along the whole coast from Castellón to Alicante.

I'd also like to show you this blog: we're building a covered pavilion for the Salesian school on Sagunto street, and I have the pleasure of observing and documenting the work for the students, so they can follow every phase even during the holidays.
