New / Nuevo / Neu

July 27, 2013

As many of you already have noticed, my posts have become less often. I have a new job! I work for a construction company in Valencia and my tasks lead me along the entire coast from Castellon to Alicante. I want to show you another thing too: this blog. We are currently building a covered pavilion for the Salesian College (Sagunto street) and I have the pleasure to observe and document the work for the students. So while they are on vacation they are able to see all the different phases of the construction work. (The website in english is under construction)
