Clothespin skier

January 26, 2013

  1. Paint a face on a ball and push it onto a toothpick.
  2. Glue the toothpick with wood glue between the two halves of a clothespin with the spring removed.
  3. Once dry, glue the clothespin ends onto two lolly sticks and let it dry again.
  4. Wrap a pipe cleaner around the body and feet for a jacket and boots.
  5. Wrap toothpicks onto the ends of the pipe cleaner as ski poles.
