Rainstick

August 12, 2015

  1. Prick holes in a spiral along a kitchen-roll tube.
  2. Push toothpicks through until they come out the other side and fix them with wood glue.
  3. When the glue is dry, snip off the protruding ends.
  4. Pour in a handful of rice or lentils and close the ends.
  5. Decorate the outside however you like: watercolours, stickers, wool…

Noah made this rainstick in his music class!
