Horchata cake

November 25, 2014

Terra i Xufa held a horchata recipe raffle and, since we're huge fans, we invented one… delicious!

  1. Mix a cup of concentrated horchata, a cup of sugar, 2 cups of flour, 3 eggs and 2 tablespoons of baking powder.
  2. Spread the batter on a greased baking tray and bake for 10 minutes at 200 degrees.
  3. Heat 100 g of butter with sliced almonds and sugar.
  4. Take the cake out, cover it with the almond mixture and bake for another 10 minutes.

That's it! And what does the Terra i Xufa team say? ;-)
