Noahs book / Noahs libro / Noahs Buch

June 2, 2015

As already indicated in this post, we'll show you Noahs book! The different skin types of still living dragon species. While I have glued the various skin types in the book, Noah has painted the dragons and and was thinking about their ability. So you won´t be distracted by the painted dragon while you are touching the skins, they are hidden behind little paper doors. Different skins to palpate: 1. Cardboard with holes 2. Wax drops 3. Corrugated 4. Fruits Packaging 5. Paper  Towel 6. Crumpled foil 7. Felt 8. Packaging Champagne moet 9. Sandpaper 10. Hamper 11. Tracing paper 12. Sponge 13. Wax
