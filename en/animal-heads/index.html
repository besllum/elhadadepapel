Animal heads

August 8, 2016

We have made a flamingo, a mouse and the head of our greyhound, after having eaten a whole sandwich. You have to glue colored paper (with wallpaper paste) to balloons. The shape of the balloons should match with the form of the animal head you wish to make. When the desired form is dire you can put on the eyes, ears by using either paint or colored paper. As you can see in the pictures, you cut a hole in the rear end to make the union with tape between the head and the piece of cardboard.
