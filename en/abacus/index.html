Abacus

February 20, 2015

A Pocket abacus! For gaining trust in numbers, which you can´t count with the fingers of your two hands, anymore. 1. Punch 10 holes, equally spaced, on the elongated sides of the matchbox (I used a piece of cork, to not imprint the box). 2. Thread a wire through a hole and thread 10 beads (5 of one color and another 5 of another) and pass the wire on the other side through the second hole back (another 10 beads ). You must repeat this 5 times. 3. Turn two ends together until they are no longer open. 4. Cut the excess 5. Press the ends down 6. Use Washitape to conceal the ends (so nobody get hurt). And now we calculating... This little box is for David !! :-)
