Sea in a bottle / en una botella / Meer in einer Flasche

February 18, 2016


Simple homemade toy for toddlers. You fill balloons with a little bit of water and air and paint a fish on it. Then push them into a water bottle with a 2/3 of water. This great toy can be even taken into the bathtub.
