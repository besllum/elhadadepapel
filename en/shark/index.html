Shark

July 5, 2015


Cut the shark and paint him. Make a cut on the continuous line and pass the wing. Glue it inside.
On the other fishes you can paste bits of foil and tie to the tail. Doing this you will find the page you are reading on for sure.
So that it won´t fall so easily from the books, you can add small pieces of magnet paper on the inner sides. Printable
