Planets / Planetas / Planeten

February 17, 2015

Ping-pong balls to fantasy planets. We got this great idea looking to what Rebecca did. Prick a couple of table tennis balls on skewers kebabs. In a small old vessel ( which you can through away ) you fill lukewarm water, and then a few drops of nail polish (various colors). These can be mixed with a toothpick. When the paint dries it gets stuck on toothpicks. (Watch out water temperature).
