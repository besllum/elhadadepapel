We have some new little people.
Stella, Julieta, Joanet, Leja and Finn are just some of the new people who can decorate your
aluminum canteen.
You choose font, color name and no one gets confused of who is the owner of the bottle.
You can find them in our online shop or in Leolo.

Tenemos nuevos personitas.
