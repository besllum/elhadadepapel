Who's looking out of the porthole?

July 21, 2016

By Sonia, Hannah and Noah.

  1. Use a paper plate as a template to draw a circle on paper and cut it out.
  2. Draw a face in the middle.
  3. Cut the centre out of the plate, leaving a hole.
  4. Paint it silver with a few black dots as screws.
  5. Glue the plate upside down onto the drawing.

I think it's a great class decoration: all the children in the same submarine, train or rocket, with their names underneath.
