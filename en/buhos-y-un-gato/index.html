Búhos y un gato / Owls and a cat / Eulen und eine Katze

January 14, 2011


You need some tubes of toilet paper. Push on one side the two edges until they meet in the middle. That is already the form of the figure with its ears. Start painting the monsters, owls, cats.... or whatever
