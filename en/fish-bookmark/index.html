Fish bookmark

November 6, 2014

In Noah's class we did a great job. A bookmark to get started with the library. 1. Cut the shape of a fish out of a cardboard. 2. Glue giant -Confetti in rows from the tail upwards towards the head. The points should protrude over the edge. 3. Cut along the fish shape. 4. Do the same on the other side 5. Punch a hole into the head and pass a yarn. 6. Knot on the other side of the thread another point (as a bubble, which you leave out of the book) 7. If you like it shiny (see image below) can put wood glue over the whole fish.
