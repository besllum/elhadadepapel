More than decor: clothespin faces

November 20, 2012

A wall decoration that helps tidy up all the loose bits of paper lying around.

  1. Cut squares of cork with a craft knife (with an adult).
  2. Paint faces: animals, people or fantasy creatures.
  3. Glue a clothespin onto each one; it can be the nose, beak, mouth…
  4. Pierce the corners and join them with wire, as in the photo.

Inspiration: Mr Printables. My first collaboration with Petit-On.
